# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is a major challenge for businesses because losing customers can directly impact revenue and long-term growth.

This project focuses on analyzing customer, subscription, and support data to identify **churn patterns, customer behavior, revenue impact, and potential factors associated with customer churn**.

The data was extracted from **Microsoft SQL Server** and analyzed using **Python in Jupyter Notebook**. Three separate datasets — Customer, Subscription, and Support — were cleaned, transformed, feature-engineered, and merged into a single analytical dataset.

The project then uses **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** and data visualization techniques to generate meaningful business insights.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

* Analyze the overall customer churn rate
* Calculate customer retention rate
* Identify churn patterns across subscription plans
* Analyze churn across different states
* Compare churn across subscription types
* Analyze revenue and user counts across different segments
* Calculate Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)
* Analyze customer age and tenure
* Identify revenue at risk due to customer churn
* Analyze customer complaints and escalation rates
* Study the relationship between support escalations and churn
* Identify patterns using exploratory data analysis and visualization

---

## 🗂️ Dataset Structure

The database consisted of three main tables:

### 1. Customer Data

Contains customer-level information such as customer details, demographics, and location.

### 2. Subscription Data

Contains information related to customer subscriptions, plans, subscription types, revenue, and tenure.

### 3. Support Data

Contains customer support-related information such as complaints and escalations.

The three datasets were cleaned and merged to create a unified dataset for analysis.

## 📊 Churn by Subscription Plan

| Plan     | Churn Rate |
| -------- | ---------: |
| Basic    | **38.46%** |
| Standard | **25.53%** |
| Premium  | **20.00%** |

### Key Insight

The **Basic plan had the highest churn rate at 38.46%**, while the Premium plan had the lowest churn rate at 20%.

This indicates that customer churn varies significantly across subscription plans and may provide an opportunity to investigate the factors affecting customers on the Basic plan.

## 🚀 Key Takeaways

The analysis provided several useful insights:

* Overall customer churn was **28.1%**
* Customer retention stood at **71.9%**
* The **Basic plan had the highest churn rate (38.46%)**
* Premium customers had the lowest churn rate among the analyzed plans
* Average customer tenure was **1,488 days**
* **₹591.66** of revenue was identified as revenue at risk from churned users
* Approximately **19.83%** of customers had escalations
* The escalation-to-churn correlation was **0.10**, suggesting only a weak linear association

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, businesses could consider:

1. **Investigating the Basic Plan**

   * Understand why customers on the Basic plan have significantly higher churn.
   * Analyze pricing, features, usage, and customer expectations.

2. **Customer Retention Strategies**

   * Identify high-risk customer segments and develop targeted retention campaigns.

3. **Revenue Protection**

   * Monitor customers contributing significantly to revenue and identify early churn signals.

4. **Support Experience Analysis**

   * Investigate customer complaints and escalations further to determine whether specific support issues contribute to customer dissatisfaction.

5. **Segment-Based Retention**

   * Develop different retention strategies based on plan, state, tenure, revenue, and customer characteristics.
